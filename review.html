8 game ideas for the tablet

Calm point-and-click / find & tinker toys in the spirit of the Moomin adventures — dragons, dinos, volcanos, space. Pick the ones you like and we build those for real.

For: boy, 5–7, learning to read (Finnish) Device: Acer Iconia B1 · Android 4.1 · 512 MB · 1024×600 · offline Rules: no speed, no timers, no flashing

Design north star

A calm tinkering toy you can’t “lose,” not a game. The child touches something and something fun happens — a sound, a motion, a reveal, a bang. The heart is cause-and-effect delight (“what if I press this?”) and the joy of finding things, with goofy moments and good audio. Text is short, big and simple so a slow reader can actually read it. Replay comes from randomness and combinations, never from levels. Art stays simple (SVG, flat color) — Moomin-storybook cosy, not Fortnite.

How to read this page

Each idea below has a live clickable prototype — hit “Try the prototype” and it loads right inside the card. These run on your Mac and are polished previews; sound starts on your first click (and may be funnier than it is pretty). Every prototype shows just one representative bit of the feel, not the whole game. The Device feasibility line on each card notes what changes for the real tablet build (ES5, no flexbox, pre-made WAV audio, cheap canvas). My top 3 are flagged.

Top 3 recommendation

★ 1st pick

06 · Salahuone The Secret Room

The truest match to the Moomin point-and-click he already loves, plus the searching he enjoys. Scales to many rooms → huge replay. Main cost is art/content (hence M–L), but it’s the heart of the brief.

★ 2nd pick

05 · Taikapata The Potion Pot

Best blend of his “test & try things” instinct, funny booms and steam, big combinatorial replay, and short readable ingredient words. Light–moderate build.

★ 3rd pick

08 · Hirviöpaja Silly Monster Maker

Lightest on the weak GPU (pure SVG), endless funny dragon/dino combos, and the generated compound names are great reading practice. A cheap, sure-win S-size piece.

Honourable mention: 01 · Tulivuori — his #1 theme and pure cause-and-effect delight; a perfect small bonus piece or hub button.
